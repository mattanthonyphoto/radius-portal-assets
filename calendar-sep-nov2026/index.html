September to November

The feed is millwork first. A team member every four to five posts, never in a cluster. The July shop day is on this calendar, spaced through finished work and Old Gravel Road.

There is not enough project photography to feature every person at that spacing before the year ends. The named people you already signed sit in the mix. The rest wait until there is more millwork to put between them. That is the next shoot.
