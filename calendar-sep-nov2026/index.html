Fourteen feet of sequenced oak

Old Gravel Road, Whistler, install in progress. What Radius calls the Cube is the rectilinear millwork at the centre of the house. Hallway wall panels in Pleasing Plank-Matched European White Oak, a CNC HVAC grill at the base, and sequenced, end-matched paneling at the entry, fourteen feet tall.
